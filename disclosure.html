Our Amazon Affiliate Disclosure

We believe in complete transparency with our readers. This disclosure policy explains the commercial relationships that exist on EverydayBeautyProducts, and how they affect the content we publish. We want you to fully understand what happens when you click links on our site and how our business model works.

1. How Amazon Affiliate Links Work

Throughout our website, you will find links to products on Amazon.com. These links are called "affiliate links." Here is exactly how they work:

  • When you click an affiliate link, you are taken directly to the product page on Amazon.com.
  • If you purchase the product within a certain time window (typically 24 hours), Amazon pays us a small referral fee — usually 1%–10% of the sale price, depending on the product category.
  • The price you pay is identical to what you would pay if you had visited Amazon directly. Our commission comes from Amazon's margin, not from any markup added to your purchase.
  • We never add hidden charges, and you are never penalized for using our affiliate links.
In Plain English: Clicking our Amazon links helps support this website at zero cost to you. The commission we earn helps us continue publishing honest, research-backed beauty content — without charging readers for access.

2. How We Choose Products to Recommend

We are deeply committed to maintaining editorial integrity. Our product selection process is based entirely on merit — not commission rates. Here is our review process:

  • Independent Research: We research each product category thoroughly, reviewing scientific literature, dermatologist opinions, and ingredient analysis.
  • Real Testing: Where possible, our editorial team tests products before recommending them.
  • Customer Review Analysis: We analyze thousands of verified Amazon reviews to identify consistent themes — both positive and negative.
  • Expert Consultation: For specific categories (e.g., anti-aging, sensitive skin), we consult licensed dermatologists and estheticians.
  • Commission-Blind Selection: Our rankings are never influenced by commission rates. A product that earns us a smaller commission can — and often does — rank higher than one with a larger payout, if it is genuinely better.

3. Sponsored Content & Brand Partnerships

Occasionally, EverydayBeautyProducts may partner with beauty brands for sponsored content. This means a brand may pay us to feature or review their product. When this occurs:

  • The content will be clearly labeled as "Sponsored" or "Paid Partnership" at the top of the article.
  • Sponsorship never guarantees a positive review. We maintain editorial independence and will note significant drawbacks even in sponsored pieces.
  • We decline partnerships with brands whose products do not meet our quality standards.
  • Sponsored content accounts for a small fraction of our total content.

4. Common Questions About Our Disclosures

Do affiliate commissions influence your ratings or rankings?

No. Our ratings and rankings are determined entirely by product quality, ingredient analysis, customer feedback, and expert opinion. Commission rates play no role in which products rank higher. We recommend products we genuinely believe in.

Are prices on your site always up to date?

We do our best to keep prices current, but Amazon prices change frequently. Always verify the final price on Amazon before purchasing. The price displayed on Amazon at checkout is the price you will pay.

Do you receive free products for review?

Occasionally brands send us complimentary products for testing. When they do, we clearly disclose this in the relevant review. Receiving a free product does not influence our rating — we maintain the same rigorous evaluation standards regardless.

Why should I trust your reviews?

Our reputation is our most valuable asset. If we recommended products that didn't perform as described, our readers would quickly lose trust in us — and our business would suffer. Our incentive is to provide genuinely useful, honest recommendations that keep readers coming back, not to chase short-term commission dollars.

How is this site legally compliant?

This disclosure complies with the Federal Trade Commission (FTC) guidelines on endorsements and testimonials (16 CFR Part 255), as well as Amazon's Operating Agreement for Associates. We take these obligations seriously and maintain full compliance.

5. FTC Compliance Statement

In accordance with the FTC's guidelines, EverydayBeautyProducts clearly discloses all material connections to products, brands, or companies that could influence our content. This includes:

  • Affiliate commissions from Amazon Associates
  • Sponsored content or paid partnerships (disclosed per-article)
  • Free products received for review (disclosed per-review)
