Common Questions

Are your product reviews genuinely unbiased? ▼
Yes. We test and review products independently. While we earn affiliate commissions from Amazon purchases (at no extra cost to you), this does not influence our ratings or recommendations. We include products we'd genuinely recommend to friends and family.
How do I suggest a product for review? ▼
We love suggestions! Use the contact form above, select "Product Recommendation Request," and tell us what you'd like us to review. We consider all suggestions and add popular requests to our review queue.
Do you accept sponsored content or paid reviews? ▼
We may occasionally partner with brands for sponsored content, which we always clearly disclose. Sponsorship never guarantees a positive review — our editorial standards remain the same regardless of the relationship.
How do affiliate links work on your site? ▼
When you click an Amazon link and make a purchase, we earn a small commission as part of the Amazon Associates program. The price you pay remains exactly the same — the commission comes from Amazon's margin, not an addition to your cost.
