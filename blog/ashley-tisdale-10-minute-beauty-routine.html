Ashley Tisdale's 10-Minute Routine for a Minimalist Summer Look

See how Ashley Tisdale builds her bare, sun-kissed summer look in just 10 minutes — simple skincare, soft glam makeup, and the everyday routine behind her glow.

Ashley Tisdale's 10-minute beauty routine proves that a full glam session isn't what makes someone look put-together. As a busy mom of two, the actress and skincare-obsessed beauty fan has landed on a routine that leans into bare, sun-kissed skin instead of heavy coverage — and it takes barely 10 minutes from start to finish.

We break down her minimalist summer look step by step, along with easy swaps you can use to recreate it at home.

The Philosophy Behind Ashley Tisdale's 10-Minute Beauty Routine

Ashley Tisdale's 10-minute routine for a minimalist summer look is built on one simple idea: skincare comes first, and makeup should enhance rather than mask. She's said that taking care of her skin is something she's done consistently since her twenties, no matter how late a night out ran. That daily consistency, more than any single product, is what makes this kind of quick routine possible in the first place.

The look she goes for in summer is deliberately relaxed — a bare, everyday finish that still feels polished enough for running errands or heading to the beach. It's a great template for anyone who wants to look effortlessly put-together without spending 45 minutes in front of a mirror every morning.

Step 1: Skincare That Sets Up the Whole Look

Before any makeup goes on, the routine starts with three quick skincare steps that do most of the heavy lifting for that bare-skin glow.

An Eye Product, Morning and Night

An eye cream or gel is applied both morning and night, a habit that's been part of the routine since her twenties. The exact formula matters less than the consistency — an antioxidant-rich, caffeine-based eye balm like the TULA Skin Care Spresso Energizing Eye Balm is a great everyday option for depuffing and hydrating the under-eye area before makeup.

This is especially important for anyone who spends a lot of time outdoors and tans easily, since sun exposure is one of the biggest accelerators of visible skin aging. How the Skincare Layer Works

Applying products in this order — eye product, SPF, then moisturizer — allows each formula to absorb properly without pilling. Lightweight products go on first, richer creams go on last, and the skin is given a moment to settle before any makeup touches the face.

As she's put it, taking care of her skin has simply always been part of her routine, something passed down from her 94-year-old grandmother — proof that consistent basics matter more than any single miracle product.

Step 2: Building a Soft, Bare-Faced Makeup Look

Once skincare is finished, the makeup portion of the routine takes about seven minutes and focuses on a few strategic, lightweight products rather than a full face of coverage.

Concealer, Only Where Needed

Rather than applying foundation, a small amount of concealer is patted only under the eyes and on any spots of discoloration. A creamy, full-coverage formula like tarte Shape Tape Concealer works well for this kind of targeted, natural application since a little goes a long way.

A Sun-Kissed Bronzer and Bright Blush

A cream or powder bronzer is swept across the high points of the face for warmth, followed by a surprisingly bright blush — proof that a bold cheek color can still read as soft and natural when blended with a larger, fluffy brush. This combination is what gives the look its "sun-kissed" finish rather than a flat, matte one.

Instead of harshly filling in the brows, a micro-feathering technique is used to mimic individual brow hairs for a soft, realistic finish. A precision pencil with a built-in spoolie, such as the Anastasia Beverly Hills Brow Definer, makes this feathered technique much easier to achieve at home.

How to Recreate This Look at Home

Want to try Ashley Tisdale's 10-minute beauty routine for yourself? Follow this simple order for the most natural, bare-faced finish:

  1. Apply an eye cream or gel around the entire eye area, patting gently rather than rubbing.
  2. Smooth on a lightweight, non-greasy SPF and allow it to absorb for a minute.
  3. Massage in a hydrating moisturizer, focusing on any dry patches.
  4. Pat concealer only where it's needed — usually just under the eyes.
  5. Sweep bronzer across the cheekbones, temples, and jawline, then add a pop of blush.
  6. Feather brow color through sparse areas using short, hair-like strokes.
  7. Draw a soft, subtle cat eye and finish with a coat of volumizing mascara.
  8. Fill lips with a skin-tone liner and top with a hydrating tinted balm.

Why the Product Order Matters

Applying skincare before makeup in this specific order — lightest formulas first, richest last — gives every layer time to settle. This is what keeps a minimalist look from sliding or separating throughout a warm summer day, and it's a habit borrowed straight from dermatologist-recommended skincare layering guidelines.

Our Final Verdict

Ashley Tisdale's 10-minute routine for a minimalist summer look is a genuinely realistic template for anyone short on time but not willing to skip skincare or a little bit of glow. It proves that a soft, bare-faced finish can look just as polished as a full face of makeup — sometimes more so — when the skin underneath is properly cared for.

Whether you stick with the exact products mentioned here or swap in the budget-friendly picks above, the real takeaway is the routine itself: consistent skincare, minimal targeted coverage, and a few strategic pops of color for warmth and glow.

Frequently Asked Questions
How long does this beauty routine actually take? ▼
True to its name, the full routine — skincare plus a soft makeup look — takes about 10 minutes once you know your product order. Skincare takes roughly 3 minutes, and the bare-faced makeup look takes about 7 minutes to complete.
What skin type is this routine best suited for? ▼
This routine is built around lightweight, non-greasy formulas, which makes it especially friendly for sensitive or breakout-prone skin. Those with very dry skin may want to layer a richer cream underneath for extra comfort.
Can I recreate this look without buying expensive products? ▼
Yes. The technique matters more than the price tag here. Budget-friendly eye creams, drugstore SPF, and affordable concealers can achieve a very similar bare, sun-kissed finish when applied the same way.
Is SPF really necessary for a quick 10-minute routine? ▼
Yes, daily SPF is one of the most important anti-aging steps you can take, even on a rushed morning. A lightweight, non-greasy sunscreen takes only seconds to apply and protects against sun damage that ages skin over time.
What's the key to getting a soft, bare-faced summer look? ▼
The key is minimal coverage layered strategically — a little concealer where needed, a soft bronzer, and a subtle cat eye rather than a full face of makeup. The goal is enhancing your natural features, not covering them up.
