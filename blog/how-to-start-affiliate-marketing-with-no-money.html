Common Questions

How to start affiliate marketing with no money on Amazon? ▼
Start by joining the free Amazon Associates program, choose a focused sub-niche such as beauty and personal care, and publish content on a free or low-cost platform. You do not need to buy products upfront — you can write informational content, build an audience, and add affiliate links once you start getting traffic.
What are the highest paying affiliate programs for beginners? ▼
Beyond Amazon Associates, the highest paying affiliate programs in the beauty and personal care niche include ShareASale and CJ Affiliate partner brands like Sephora, Ulta, and Dermstore, along with LTK (RewardStyle), which often pay 4 to 10 percent commission, higher than Amazon's standard rates.
Can I do affiliate marketing for beginners with free websites? ▼
Yes. Free affiliate marketing websites built on platforms like WordPress.com, Blogger, or a free subdomain can get you started immediately. As your traffic grows, migrating to a self-hosted WordPress site with a custom domain improves your long-term SEO and revenue potential.
How long does it take to reach six figures in affiliate marketing? ▼
With consistent execution following a 12-month content and SEO plan, many beauty and personal care affiliate sites can realistically reach six-figure annual revenue within 12 to 24 months, depending on publishing frequency, niche competitiveness, and monetization diversification.
